keybinds: every word the cheatsheet, settings panel, palette and context menu show comes from a `strings` option
(`defaultStrings`, in English, for the rest), as do the inputs' (`rebindKeys()` and the others) and the formats'
(`formatKey()`, `bindingParts()` and the others, last). The palette's `label` and `placeholder` and the menu's `label`
are now `strings.commands`, `strings.typeCommand` and `strings.actions`.
